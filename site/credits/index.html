Reference projects

Twenty open-source projects Global Intelligence Atlas learns from for visuals, presentation, frameworks and modelling theory. No code is copied from them; each licence is compatible with AGPL-3.0-only. Full APA citations are in docs/REFERENCES.md.

ProjectLicenceWhat Atlas takes from it
CesiumJS (Cesium GS)Apache-2.0Reference for virtual-globe design: terrain under a globe, time-dynamic scenes driven by one clock, and the timeline widget that inspired the Replay slider.
deck.gl (vis.gl, OpenJS Foundation)MITReference for batching very large point and trip layers on the graphics card in a few draw calls, as Atlas does for aircraft and ships.
kepler.glMITReference for time-filtered playback of geospatial data with a play button and a time slider (the Replay layer).
MapLibre GL JSBSD-3-ClauseReference for vector tiles, label placement and globe projection in an open map renderer.
3DTilesRendererJS (NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, NASA-AMMOS)Apache-2.0Reference for streaming 3D terrain and buildings into three.js by level of detail, for the street-level and 3D surroundings views.
Apache EChartsApache-2.0Reference for clear, animated dashboard charts (feed health and counts).
D3 (Data-Driven Documents)ISCReference for map projections, scales and colour ramps (Bostock et al., 2011).
Observable PlotISCReference for the grammar of graphics: small, honest charts with labelled axes for Learn cards.
OpenLayersBSD-2-ClauseReference for handling many tile sources and coordinate reference systems side by side.
reveal.js (Hakim El Hattab)MITReference for presentation flow: step-by-step guided tours with keyboard control.
H3: hexagonal hierarchical geospatial index (Uber)Apache-2.0Reference for equal-area hexagonal binning to summarise dense points such as fires and aircraft.
Turf.jsMITReference for geodesic measures (distance, bearing, point in polygon) used in watch areas and flight paths.
Supercluster (Mapbox)ISCReference for fast hierarchical point clustering by zoom level (marker decluttering).
PROJ (OSGeo)MITReference for coordinate transformations, such as the local east-north frame used to place terrain and buildings.
FilterPy (Roger Labbe)MITModelling theory: Kalman filtering, the principled form of the dead-reckoning that glides aircraft between position reports (Kalman, 1960).
traffic (Xavier Olive)MITModelling theory: aircraft trajectory processing and analysis from ADS-B data (Olive, 2019).
MovingPandas (Anita Graser)BSD-3-ClauseModelling theory: movement data as trajectories over time, the model behind the hourly Replay frames (Graser, 2019).
Skyfield (Brandon Rhodes)MITModelling theory: accurate positions of the Sun, Moon and satellites, used to check the app’s own formulas.
OSINT Framework (Justin Nordine)MITReference for organising open-source intelligence tools as a browsable tree (the Intel directory).
NetworkXBSD-3-ClauseModelling theory: graph and network analysis for cables, pipelines, power and trade routes (Hagberg et al., 2008).
