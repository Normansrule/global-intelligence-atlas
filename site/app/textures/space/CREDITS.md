| mimas.jpg, enceladus.jpg, tethys.jpg, dione.jpg, rhea.jpg, iapetus.jpg, miranda.jpg, ariel.jpg, umbriel.jpg, titania.jpg, oberon.jpg, triton.jpg, titan.jpg (v2.21) | Global maps of the big moons of Saturn, Uranus and Neptune from Cassini and Voyager images (where a moon was never photographed, the gap is filled with plain grey); Titan's is a rendering of its orange haze, not a map of its surface | [NASA 3D Resources](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/tree/master/Images%20and%20Textures) | Public domain (NASA media guidelines) |

Changes made: resized where noted (the v2.21 moon maps and phobos.jpg to 1024 × 512) and re-saved; Titan's haze toned down towards its true colour; on vesta.jpg the unmapped north (black) filled with grey and a credit line printed on the map painted over (the credit is here); nothing else.

Shapes (site/app/data/shapes, v2.22): radius maps made by `tools/build-smallworlds.py` from Stellarium's shape models of Phobos, Deimos and Vesta (models/m1phobos.obj.gz, m2deimos.obj.gz, 4vesta_21_MLfix.obj; GNU General Public License 2.0 or later) and NASA 3D Resources' shape model of Bennu (3D Printing/Asteroid 101955 Bennu, from OSIRIS-REx; public domain). The atlas also loads, only when you fly close, NASA's own global mosaics from [Solar System Treks](https://trek.nasa.gov) (public domain); they are not stored here.
