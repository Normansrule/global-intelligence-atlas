Moon theories compiled into C by Johannes Gajdosik for Stellarium (src/core/planetsephems/)

Global Intelligence Atlas translates three of these files into JavaScript:
  - tass17.c   (TASS 1.7, Saturn's moons; Vienne & Duriez, 1995)  -> site/app/js/outermoons.js (v2.21)
  - gust86.c   (GUST86, Uranus's moons; Laskar & Jacobson, 1987)  -> site/app/js/outermoons.js (v2.21)
  - marssat.c  (Lainey's ephemerides of Phobos and Deimos)       -> site/app/js/smallworlds.js (v2.22)
The term tables were converted by tools/build-outermoons.py and tools/build-smallworlds.py.
The surrounding code (the rest of the atlas) is AGPL-3.0-only; these parts keep the notice below.
